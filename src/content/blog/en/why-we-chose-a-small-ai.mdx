When most people hear "AI", they picture a giant chatbot that can write a poem, plan a trip and explain black holes, all before lunch. Impressive!

But when we started designing CycleBuddy, we asked a much simpler question: **what does a period app actually need an AI to do?**

The answer turned out to be small. So we built small.

## Big AI lives far away

Big AI models are, well, big. Too big to fit on a phone. So they live in huge data centers, and every question you ask has to travel over the internet to a company's servers, get answered there, and travel back.

That's fine when you're asking for a banana bread recipe. It's a very different story when the question is about your period dates, your cramps, your mood or your sex life: some of the most personal information you have.

## Small AI lives with you

A model that only has one job can be small enough to live **inside the app, on your phone**. It learns from what you log, right there. It works even with no internet at all. And your cycle never has to leave your phone for the app to be useful.

## One job, done really well

A big AI is a generalist: it knows a little about everything. Ours is a specialist. It has exactly one job: **understand your cycle and help you care for it.**

So instead of guessing from the phase of your cycle alone, it looks at what *you* logged (say, cramps at 6 out of 10, low energy and a craving for salt) and suggests what might help: a warm meal, gentle movement, a heat pad, and why.

Think of it as the difference between searching the whole internet and opening a pocket guide written just for you.

<Callout title="The honest trade-off">

Our AI won't write your wedding speech or help with your taxes. It isn't a chatbot that knows everything, and that's on purpose.

A focused model is also easier to check: we can test the one thing it does again and again, instead of hoping a giant model gets it right.

## Small, on purpose

We didn't choose small because it was easier. We chose it because your body's data deserves to stay yours. Small, local, private and aligned with your wellbeing: that's the kind of AI women deserve, so it's the one we're building.
