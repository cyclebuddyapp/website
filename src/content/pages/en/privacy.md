## The short version

- This website uses **no cookies, no analytics and no trackers**.
- We only receive information **you choose to send us** through a form.
- We use it only for the reason you sent it, and we never sell it.
- You can ask us to delete it at any time: write to [hello@cyclebuddy.ca](mailto:hello@cyclebuddy.ca).

## What the forms collect, and why

| Form | What we collect | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waitlist | Your email, your language and, if you choose, your country | To tell you when CycleBuddy launches and share occasional news, in your language |
| Contact | Your name, email and message | To read your message and reply to you |
| Research partners | Your name, institution, email and message | To discuss a possible research partnership |

Each form also has a hidden field that people never see. It helps us block automated spam. If it's filled in, the message is discarded.

## Who handles your information

To send and store form messages, we use two service providers:

- **Cloudflare** runs the small server function that receives the forms. It uses your IP address briefly to limit abuse (for example, too many submissions in a short time). We don't store it.
- **Resend**, an email service based in the United States, delivers form messages to our inbox and keeps the waitlist so we can email you.

This website itself is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We don't have access to those logs and we don't use them.

The fonts on this site are served from our own domain, so loading a page doesn't contact any third party.

## How long we keep it

- **Waitlist:** until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link.
- **Contact and research messages:** as long as needed to answer you and follow up, then deleted.

## Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information you sent us. Write to [hello@cyclebuddy.ca](mailto:hello@cyclebuddy.ca) and we'll take care of it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date below. If we ever add analytics, they will be cookieless and self-hosted, and this page will say so first.
